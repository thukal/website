Our flagship

Collections as they actually happen — at the door.

Vasool

Voice-entry loan collection. A collection agent standing at a customer's door speaks the amount in their own language and moves on — no typing, no signal needed, no evening spent copying a notebook into a computer.

  • Voice-entry daily collections, in regional languages
  • Works fully offline, syncs when the phone finds signal
  • GPS route management and field staff tracking
  • Daily, weekly, gold, property and chit-fund loans
  • AI credit scoring before you lend
  • Audit trails, KYC storage, PDF receipts, 12+ reports

Built to scale with you — from a single money lender with one route, to a multi-branch finance company, to a regulated NBFC.

    Manitham HRMS

    Nine modules covering employees, attendance, leave, payroll and records. Punches arrive by biometric device, GPS or browser; payroll works out PF, ESI and TDS, with every line explaining its own arithmetic. A WhatsApp assistant answers the questions staff would otherwise ask HR. Month-end is generate, lock, pay.

    hrms.work
  • Our product

    Nalam

    One platform, three systems — hospital, laboratory and clinic. A patient goes from registration to discharge without paper: appointments and doctor availability, test orders and sample tracking, pharmacy stock with expiry, itemised bills and insurance claims. Lab instruments report into it directly, and KK AI answers questions about the day in plain language.
