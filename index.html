What we do

  • Custom business software

    The system your operation actually runs on — billing, inventory, scheduling, reporting. Built around how you already work, not around a template.

  • Web and mobile apps

    Customer-facing products that hold up on a patchy connection and a three-year-old phone. Designed, built, and shipped to the stores.

  • Idea to first version

    You have a product in your head. We turn it into something real people can use in weeks, then keep cutting until only what matters is left.

  • Keeping it running

    Hosting, backups, monitoring, and the unglamorous fixes. Software you don't have to think about is the point.
